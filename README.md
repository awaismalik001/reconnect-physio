# 🏥 Reconnect Physiotherapy Center
### *Next-Generation Clinical Operations & Practice Management Platform*
> **Reconnect Physio** transforms everyday clinic workflows into a seamless, high-efficiency digital experience—empowering practitioners to focus on what matters most: **patient recovery.**
---
### ✨ Highlights & Core Features
* **📋 Centralized Patient Records & History**
  * Instant access to comprehensive digital profiles, chief complaints, and diagnosis logs.
  * Direct file attachments for medical reports, MRI scans, X-rays, and prescriptions.
  * Smart tracking for treatment duration, emergency contacts, and assigned physiotherapists.
* **📅 Smart Scheduling & Doctor Management**
  * Manage therapist profiles, specializations, and availability in real time.
  * Frictionless appointment booking with live daily schedules and status tracking.
* **🎯 Detailed Session Tracking & Recovery Milestones**
  * Log therapy modalities on the fly (*Manual Therapy, Dry Needling, Electrotherapy, and more*).
  * Record clinical observations, patient recovery metrics, and targeted home exercises.
  * Maintain complete session history from initial intake to final discharge.
* **💳 Intelligent Finance & Credit Ledger**
  * Real-time cash flow overview: track session revenues, clinic expenses, and net profit.
  * Dedicated **Credit Ledger** to easily monitor, follow up on, and settle outstanding balances.
  * Multi-method payment logging (Cash, Card, Online Transfer).
* **📄 1-Click Automated PDF Invoicing**
  * Generate polished, itemized, and branded patient billing receipts instantly.
  * Clean layout optimized for clinic records and patient reimbursement submissions.
* **⚡ Ultra-Fast, Secure & Scalable Architecture**
  * **Frontend:** React 18, Vite, and Tailwind CSS for a fluid, responsive UI.
  * **Backend:** Node.js & Express REST API with robust JWT authentication.
  * **Database:** MongoDB for agile, reliable, and high-performance data storage.
